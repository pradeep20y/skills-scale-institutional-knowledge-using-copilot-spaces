# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

## Overview

OctoAcme manages work through a structured, iterative project lifecycle designed to maximize customer value while maintaining clarity, accountability, and continuous learning. The team focuses on validating needs early, planning incremental delivery, tracking execution with visibility, and improving through regular retrospectives.

This documentation provides the core processes, decision points, and guidance used to lead work from initiation through release and continuous improvement.

## Core principles

- Customer-first delivery: prioritize outcomes that create measurable value for the user.
- Iterative execution: break work into small, testable increments that can be reviewed and adjusted quickly.
- Clear ownership: assign responsibility for decisions, progress, and outcomes.
- Data-informed decision making: use evidence, metrics, and feedback to guide trade-offs.
- Psychological safety and learning: encourage open communication, feedback, and improvement.

## OctoAcme project lifecycle

OctoAcme follows a practical project management flow built around five major phases:

1. Initiation
   - Validate the business need, align stakeholders, and decide whether to authorize the work.
2. Planning
   - Define scope, milestones, backlog, dependencies, and the path to delivery.
3. Execution & Tracking
   - Build, review, test, and monitor progress against plan while adjusting as needed.
4. Risk & Communication
   - Identify issues, escalate blockers, and keep stakeholders informed with clear updates.
5. Release & Deployment
   - Deliver the work safely, verify adoption or impact, and close out the release.
6. Retrospective & Continuous Improvement
   - Capture lessons learned and improve the process for future work.

This lifecycle creates a consistent rhythm for project teams while preserving flexibility for product and delivery needs.

## Team operating model

OctoAcme projects typically include a cross-functional team with clear partnership between product and delivery functions. The project manager helps maintain execution rhythm and communication, while product leadership guides prioritization and outcomes. Developers, QA, and stakeholders all contribute to delivery and quality in a collaborative environment.

For detailed role definitions, see [OctoAcme Roles and Personas](octoacme-roles-and-personas.md).

## Summary

The OctoAcme project management approach is intentionally simple and scalable: define the business need, plan work clearly, execute in small increments, communicate risks and progress early, release with confidence, and continuously improve based on evidence and team reflection. This documentation is designed as a central entry point for that operating model.
